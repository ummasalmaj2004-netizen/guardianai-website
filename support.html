Support & Reporting

Clear, calm steps for reporting abuse, preserving evidence, and reaching emergency help.

Steps to report abuse

  1. Stay calm - it is not your fault.
  2. Save evidence before deleting anything.
  3. Block the person or account.
  4. Use the platform's Report feature (GuardianAI can guide you screen-by-screen).
  5. Tell a trusted adult, teacher, or support service.

Evidence collection guide

  • Screenshot messages, usernames, dates, and links.
  • Do not edit or forward harmful content.
  • Store copies privately and securely.

Emergency support

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services right away.

Digital Safety Learning Hub

Short, youth-friendly lessons that build confidence and digital rights awareness. Open a topic to start learning.

Online Privacy

Passwords, privacy settings, sharing safely, location and photos.

  • Use long, unique passwords and turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Check who can see your posts, location, and contact details.
  • Think before sharing photos - screenshots can travel far and fast.

Cyberbullying Awareness

Recognise bullying, respond safely, support friends, and get help.

  • Bullying is repeated, targeted harm - it is never your fault.
  • Do not retaliate. Save evidence, block, report, and tell a trusted adult.
  • Support friends: a kind message and standing beside them really helps.

Formats to add: bite-size lessons - quizzes - videos - downloadable guides.

Scam Prevention

Phishing, fake prizes, impersonation, and too-good-to-be-true offers.

  • Prizes that ask for passwords, codes, or fees are scams.
  • Urgency ('act now!') is a pressure tactic - slow down and verify.
  • Go to the official app or website yourself instead of clicking message links.

Formats to add: bite-size lessons - quizzes - videos - downloadable guides.

Digital Rights

Your rights online: safety, expression, privacy, and participation.

  • You have the right to be safe and free from harassment online.
  • You have the right to privacy and to control your personal information.
  • You have the right to participate, learn, and express yourself respectfully.

Formats to add: bite-size lessons - quizzes - videos - downloadable guides.

Technology-Facilitated Violence Awareness

Harassment, threats, image-based abuse, and stalking - and where to get support.

  • Threats, stalking, and sharing private images without consent are forms of abuse.
  • You never have to handle it alone - trusted adults and support services can help.
  • Preserve evidence safely and use platform reporting and blocking tools.

AI Risk Detection

Goal: a young person can verify a suspicious message in seconds — and always leaves knowing what to do next. The brief's phishing example is pre-loaded.

  1. 1

    Text input box. Paste-first design with an on-device privacy note; a link checker and screenshot upload share the same entry row.

User Flowchart

Scenario: a young person receives a harmful online message. Select any step to read what the user sees, what GuardianAI does, and why the step exists. Two paths always stay open: an emergency fast-path to Report & Support from every step, and a "not sure yet" path into the Learning Hub.

alt: "Not sure" → Learning Hub
YES — RISK FOUND
NO — NO CLEAR RISK

— both paths converge —

Emergency fast-path (available at every step): if the AI detects immediate danger — threats of physical harm, self-harm signals, or an adult soliciting a minor — GuardianAI interrupts the flow and routes straight to Report & Support · Emergency, with local helplines and a "stay on this page while you call" option.

A harmful message arrives

Entry point · outside GuardianAI

  1. U

    User sees: A threatening, mocking or manipulative message — e.g. "Someone is threatening me online." Fear, embarrassment or confusion are the entry emotions; the design must never add blame.

  2. G

    GuardianAI: Not yet involved. This moment defines the requirement: help must be findable in one search, open without sign-up, and work on a shared or low-end phone.

  3. D

    Design decision: Every later screen is written for a distressed reader: short sentences, large tap targets, no jargon, no account wall.

Tip: select steps in the flow — including the decision diamond — to inspect the reasoning behind each one.

Storyboard

One story, eight scenes: how GuardianAI turns a frightening moment into confidence. The emotion meter under each scene tracks the user's state across the journey — from distressed to a confident digital citizen.

Amara, 16 — student

Uses a shared family phone after school. Active on social media and class group chats. Represents GuardianAI's primary user: connected, social, and exposed to risks adults rarely see.

GuardianAI — the companion

A calm, non-judgemental AI assistant in the shape of a shield. It explains before it instructs, never blames the user, and always connects advice to a concrete next action.

05

Design Principles

Six commitments run through every wireframe, flow and scene above — the values stakeholders from UNFPA, UNDP, UNICEF and youth innovation programmes asked this design to represent.

Youth empowerment

Young people are decision-makers, not victims: every flow ends with a skill learned and a step the user took themselves.

Digital safety

Identify, advise, act. Risk is always named in plain words, levelled honestly, and paired with a concrete next action.

Trust & privacy

Anonymous by default, on-device first analysis, delete-anything-anytime. A child in crisis is never the product.

AI innovation

The AI shows its reasoning — signals detected, confidence, why — so young users learn to spot risks themselves.

Accessibility

Readable type (Atkinson Hyperlegible), voice input, audio lessons, risk levels never shown by colour alone, low-bandwidth first.

Inclusivity

Multiple languages, diverse stories in the storyboard and lessons, and technology-facilitated violence covered without stigma.

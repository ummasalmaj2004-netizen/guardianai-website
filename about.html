About GuardianAI

GuardianAI: An Intelligent Digital Safety Companion for Youth Protection - developed for the SafeTech Africa Hacklab Youth Social Innovation Challenge 2026.

Our mission

Helping youth identify online risks, understand digital rights, and stay safer in digital spaces.

How it works

  1. A young person describes a problem or pastes a suspicious message.
  2. GuardianAI analyses the situation and identifies possible risks.
  3. It gives step-by-step safety guidance and reporting actions.
  4. Learning resources build long-term confidence and awareness.

Design principles

Youth empowerment

Young people are partners and leaders in digital safety.

Digital safety

Practical, calm guidance that helps youth act early and confidently.

Trust & privacy

Anonymous by default and transparent about data.

AI innovation

AI that explains its reasoning and keeps humans in the loop.

Accessibility

Readable language and mobile-first, low-bandwidth friendly pages.

Inclusivity

Designed with diverse youth across languages, genders, and abilities.

Trust, safety & safeguarding

  • GuardianAI provides general safety guidance, not legal or medical advice.
  • Serious risks are always escalated towards trusted adults and professional services.
  • Emergency information must use verified local helplines for each launch country.
